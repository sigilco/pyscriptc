What does pyscriptc support?

pyscriptc compiles a typed subset of Python 3.14 to native code. This page is the honest answer: what compiles, what stops at a named fence, and what runs but behaves differently from CPython. Measured, not claimed.

Divergences from CPython 3.14

Every numbered difference from CPython, in the style of docs/reference/divergences.md. Anything not listed here is claimed identical; a difference you find that is not listed is a bug.

Language features

compat2 probe groups over the visible corpus. The Failures column is the one that matters: probes that ran and did not match CPython. Look a fence code up with pyscriptc explain PSxxxx.

Builtins

One row per probed group of Python builtins. “Not supported” lists the forms that stop at a fence. Pass rate: the compat2 probes covering the name, out of those that ran.

Standard library

One row per probed module. “Not supported” lists the module surface that stops at a fence. Pass rate: the compat2 probes covering the module, out of those that ran.

PyPI top 100

The pinned top-100 packages, measured with pyscriptc coverage. Status: no fences is full, at most half the definitions fenced is partial, more is none.

Fence census

Fence codes over the measured packages, from the same run as the table above. The fix is the compiler’s own remediation hint (pyscriptc explain PSxxxx).

Targets

Where a compiled program can run. Native is built and smoke tested in CI, experimental works with known gaps, and every row names the lane that proves it. Cross-compilation goes through zig cc (pyscriptc compile --target <triple>).

History

One point per nightly measurement. One point means the trend just started.
