Adoption ladder

One rate per rung of the adoption ladder, measured against CPython recordings: a single-file script (R1), a multi-module tree (R2), and the dependency lanes the locked top-100 trees reach (R4). There is never one overall “Python compat percent”.

Lane shares

How the third-party imports of the pinned trees resolve by lane today. native compiles from source, pack swaps in a curated replacement, extern goes through a C ABI manifest, island is the opt-in embedded-CPython tier, absent compiles the ImportError branch.

Language features

compat2 probe groups over the visible corpus. Weighted pass is the scorer’s weighted share of passing probes. Look a fence code up with pyscriptc explain PSxxxx.

PyPI top 100

The pinned top-100 packages, measured with pyscriptc coverage. Status: no fences is full, at most half the definitions fenced is partial, more is none.
